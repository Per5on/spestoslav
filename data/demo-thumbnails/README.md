# Sample thumbnail catalogue

Nine local sample images: three preserved Preview003 grocery references and six licensed Commons technology photos. Metadata records source, author, licence and derivative status; assets are in backend/app/demo_assets. These are sample illustrations, not verified images of real catalogue SKUs. No barcode or price is assigned.

Commons attribution/ShareAlike terms remain linked in metadata and exposed in the app About credits. Reuse the thumbnails under their recorded licences; preserve the attribution and original source reference. Sample mode is opt-in; public catalogue cache and price comparison exclude demos.
