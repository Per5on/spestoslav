# Representative catalogue thumbnail pilot

The 160 × 140 WebPs were resized and placed on a white canvas. The produce photos illustrate unbranded produce, with bindings scoped to the verified Burlex catalogue codes listed below. The Galaxy photograph illustrates the device model. A photograph of the device cannot establish storage capacity, regional specification, condition, or merchant inventory.
